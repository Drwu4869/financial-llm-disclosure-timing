DECISION RULE
If the old errors largely disappear under this configuration, the old
practical failure claim must be narrowed. The algebraic weakness of
homogeneous tests alone does not establish a difficult real-world task.
If exact fields plus a calculator succeed while model totals fail, the
remaining evidence concerns arithmetic and pipeline design.
If field errors remain, use the complete failure census; a few cases
do not establish a general extraction mechanism.
