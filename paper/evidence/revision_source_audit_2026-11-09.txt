https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2025/ic-35538.pdf

Assessing Look-Ahead Bias in Stock Return Predictions Generated By GPT Sentiment Analysis.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.17322

The Memorization Problem: Can We Trust LLMs' Economic Forecasts?
https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=201880

Temporal Leakage in LLM Backtesting: Measurement, Validation, and Adjusted Scores.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02985

Benhenda, Mostapha (2026).
Look-Ahead-Bench: a Standardized Benchmark of Look-ahead Bias in Point-in-Time LLMs for Finance.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13770

Wang, Chong et al. (2025).
LLMs Meet Library Evolution: Evaluating Deprecated API Usage in LLM-Based Code Completion.

https://arxiv.org/html/2406.09834v3

Used as an analogy for version-sensitive code generation, not evidence about
the internal origin of the particular financial-rule error in this paper.

Additional source check for the editorial restructure (9 November 2026)

SEC 2024: Form N-PORT and Form N-CEN Reporting; Guidance on Open-End Fund
Liquidity Risk Management Programs, Release IC-35308, adopted 28 August 2024.
https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2024/ic-35308.pdf
Printed pages 14 and 32 distinguish monthly filing within 30 days from public
release after 60 days. The later IC-35538 delay release controls implementation
dates. The discussion's day-30/day-60 example is an analytical scenario, not
a new measured model failure or a statement that this regime is already live.

SEC 2026: Form N-PORT Reporting, proposed rule, IC-35962, 18 February 2026.
https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/proposed/2026/ic-35962.pdf
Table 1, printed page 9: pre-amendment third-month publication upon filing;
2024 adopted schedule monthly filing in 30 days and publication at day 60;
2026 proposal filing in 45 days and third-month-only publication at day 60.
Official status page checked 9 November 2026:
https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/02/s7-2026-05
The page labels the action Proposed Rule. The manuscript does not present the
proposal as operative law or assume the 2024 amendments will begin unchanged.

Inference kept explicit: using verified public availability remains valid
under rule changes. Equating a raw submission timestamp with public availability
can fail when publication and filing are separated. The P2 fixture contract
supplies the public date, so the experiment does not show P2 itself failing.
