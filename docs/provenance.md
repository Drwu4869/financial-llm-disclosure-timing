# Provenance, status, and public export

## What this release is

The current manuscript and its saved-evidence audits were assembled before this GitHub preparation. The research narratives, decision log, navigation, and release-check scripts were prepared on 9 November 2026. They are retrospective documentation, not original freeze records. No model answers were added for this publication step.

Original study freezes are local timestamp/hash records. They are not registrations with an independent third party. Model outputs belong to the product labels and settings recorded in the corresponding stage; mutable commercial interfaces prevent an exact backend-snapshot replication claim.

## Different kinds of evidence

- **Controlled synthetic tasks:** portfolio descriptions, constructed accounts, disclosure events, and code fixtures. These permit exact answers under stated assumptions; they are not sampled customer portfolios.
- **Issuer-derived inputs:** published holdings percentages and report text, combined with constructed account allocations. Table extraction and in-project visual checks do not imply independent human annotation.
- **Public SEC metadata:** archived SUBMISSION tables and accession-level derivations. These are real filing records; the extra waiting is a mechanical counterfactual.
- **Model evidence:** saved first responses, technical recovery series, candidate source, and isolated execution outputs. Seeds and interventions are not independent model generations.
- **Mathematics and controls:** authored error classes and reference policies. Their outputs are never counted as model responses.

AI assistance supported design, implementation, collection, analysis, source checking, and writing. No blinded independent human replication or legal expert certification is asserted. Historical documents retain their original descriptions; later records qualify earlier claims where needed.

## Public-export changes

The publication copy omits operational browser accessibility dumps, login/capture screenshots, rendering QA caches, and Python caches. Private chat-session URLs in collection metadata are replaced with `[private-session-url-redacted]`. Candidate program source and saved experimental output bytes are not edited. The full audit is in [public_export_changes.json](../evidence/public_export_changes.json).

ZIP files, including nested packages, may be recompressed. Original package/file hashes are retained for provenance; they must not be confused with hashes of the distributed export. Use [release_manifest.json](../release_manifest.json) for this repository's bytes. Frozen scientific-input hashes and candidate/source/output hashes are checked by the original audit scripts. Original internal whole-package manifests may include omitted or redacted operational files.

Historical reports and bundles are explicitly historical. For example, the 5 November portfolio manuscripts predate the 6 November reasoning recheck. Their original conclusions must be interpreted with that subsequent evidence, rather than presented as current strong-model results.
