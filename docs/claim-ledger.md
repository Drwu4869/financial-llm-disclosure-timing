## Claims not established

- A universal financial framing or diversification bias.
- A new universally effective correctness certificate or arithmetic algorithm.
- A causal estimate of the benefit of thinking alone.
- A general ranking of GPT, Gemini, Claude, or local models.
- A complete two-vendor 88-answer rule panel.
- A validated method for reconstructing historical public availability from missing timestamps.
- The economic profitability of prompt P2, or a measured trading loss from the floor.
- Acceptance, peer review, independent legal certification, or a guarantee of research novelty.

## Denominator conventions

An independent generation unit is a first answer, not each seed, daily vector, or perturbation. Primary, recovery, development, and post-hoc observations retain their labels. Missing answers are not wrong answers. Interface failures and intervention refusals are not automatic passes. Current metadata and synthetic experimental counts refer to different data sources and purposes.
