## Export and integrity

[Artifact inventory](../evidence/artifact_inventory.json) records original-source and exported ZIP hashes. [Public-export changes](../evidence/public_export_changes.json) records omitted browser traces and redacted private chat URLs. Model source and saved experimental outputs are not modified. Nested ZIP recompression changes archive hashes even when member content is unchanged.

[release_manifest.json](../release_manifest.json) is the authoritative hash list for this distributed repository. The original internal package manifests are historical records and may list operational files deliberately excluded from the public export.
